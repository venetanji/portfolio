Papinee World is a multi-sensory experience for children, created in collaboration with Papinee and exhibited at Pearl Lam Galleries in Hong Kong. The installation comprises a set of interactive stations where children explore the characters and stories of a magical world through projection screens, narration and sound effects rendered in ambisonic audio.
